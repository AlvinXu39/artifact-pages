The shape of it, before the detail

Background

The news is a market-share and pricing signal, not a tariff change. Eurostat figures show Bangladesh selling less into the EU and at a lower average unit price while China and Vietnam improved their position.

What's being affected

The exposure sits in EU-bound apparel sourcing, especially orders where buyers can compare Bangladesh price, China scale, Vietnam reliability and alternative suppliers. Woven and knit basic programs are the easiest to benchmark.

Likely impact

A 7.43% unit-price fall can reopen price negotiations even without a formal duty change. Buyers use the data to press suppliers for sharper pricing, proof of reliability and clearer capacity commitments.

The factory question is whether this is your direct margin shock, a competitive opening, or a buyer-benchmarking risk.
