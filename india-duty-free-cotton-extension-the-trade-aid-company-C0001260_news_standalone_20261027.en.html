The shape of it, before the detail

Background

This is an input-cost and sourcing-confidence event. India removed the 11% cotton import duty during the lean season, and sources said ministries are expected to keep that relief through December.

What's being affected

The rule affects imported cotton used by Indian mills and downstream cotton-heavy fabric and apparel programs. It does not directly cut FOB costs in Bangladesh, Pakistan, Hong Kong, Mauritius, the UK or the US.

Likely impact

Domestic cotton prices were above ₹9,000 per 100 kg, while output was expected below demand. The duty relief is large enough to reopen cotton-cost discussions, but rupee, freight and futures can absorb part of it.

The factory question is whether this lowers your own cotton input cost, weakens your price position against India, or only changes buyer comparisons.
