EU chemical limits are live. PFHxA proof matters. Buyers ask earlier.

The EU now restricts PFHxA, its salts and related substances under REACH Annex XVII for consumer products. PFHxA and salts at or above 25 ppb, or PFHxA-related substances at or above 1,000 ppb, cannot be placed on the market in covered goods. Clothing, related accessories and footwear for the general public are the key apparel categories.

The shape of it, before the detail

Background

PFHxA is a PFAS chemistry used for water, oil or stain resistance. REACH Annex XVII now sets sale and use limits for covered consumer goods.

What's being affected

The apparel lane is EU-bound clothing, accessories and footwear. Labs measure the substance in homogeneous material, so fabric, coating, trim or finish records matter.

Likely impact

This is less about a price jump and more about release risk. Missing test proof can delay shipment approval even when the factory did not choose the chemistry.

The practical split is between suppliers that can show declarations now and suppliers that must chase upstream chemistry data.

From rule to factory action

The rule creates a documentation split before it creates a sourcing shift.
