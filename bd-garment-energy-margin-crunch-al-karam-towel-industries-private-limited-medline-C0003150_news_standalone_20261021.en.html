The shape of it, before the detail

Background

This is an operating-cost and reliability event, not a buyer-border duty. Energy inflation changes factory margin, production scheduling and the confidence buyers need before placing time-sensitive orders.

What's being affected

The strongest exposure is Bangladesh garment production that relies on gas, diesel, generators or unstable utility supply. Suppliers outside Bangladesh feel the event through buyer comparisons on delivery risk and cost stability.

Likely impact

A move from roughly $200,000 to $300,000 in one factory’s daily energy bill is large enough to change margin and financing conversations. Buyers will ask who can absorb cost without risking shipment dates.

The factory question is whether energy cost hits your own margin or creates a competitor-risk opening.
