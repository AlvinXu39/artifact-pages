Canada counter-tariffs affected selected US imports. Goods inside the tariff list face a landed-cost increase; goods outside the list or sourced elsewhere avoid the direct duty. The pressure is active now.

The shape of it, before the detail

Background

This is a tariff-cost event. Duties change the landed price at import and can alter buyer sourcing discussions even when the factory's own production cost is unchanged. The effect depends on HS code, origin, importer of record and contract terms (LIVE).

What's being affected

The clearest exposure is US-origin goods entering Canada under covered tariff lines. Non-US origin supply can become more attractive if product qualification, compliance and delivery stay comparable (HYPOTHESIS).

Likely impact

The gap is large enough to reopen landed-cost comparisons for covered goods. It does not automatically move every order because qualification, tooling, buyer approval and delivery reliability still matter.

The split is tariff exposure, sourcing origin and buyer switching ability.

Tariff impact path

Tariffs create winners and losers by product code and origin. Suppliers outside the direct tariff can still feel buyer questions.
