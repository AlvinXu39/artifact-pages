Bhiwandi yarn costs jumped. Fabric margins broke. Buyers test quotes.

Apparel Views reported that Bhiwandi yarn prices rose 60–70% in six months, while finished grey-fabric prices did not rise in line. Powerloom and grey-fabric units exposed to yarn buying face margin pressure; suppliers outside that input squeeze gain a quote-stability opening.

The shape of it, before the detail

Background

Bhiwandi is a major Maharashtra powerloom and textile centre. The article names a raw-material shock: yarn moved sharply higher, but finished grey fabric still sells below production cost for some manufacturers.

What's being affected

The pressure runs through yarn, weaving, grey fabric, processing, trading, transport and working capital. Named yarn varieties include micro, full dull, polyester, magic and cotton yarn, with several price jumps reported since March.

Likely impact

This is not a tariff event. The commercial effect is a cost pass-through test: buyers will ask whether India-linked fabric and garment prices are temporary, negotiable or a sign of supplier cash stress.

The factory question is whether this is your own input squeeze, a competitor opening, or only a buyer benchmark.
